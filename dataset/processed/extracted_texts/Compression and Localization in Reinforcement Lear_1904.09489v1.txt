5 RelatedWork
ThesuccessofDeepQnetworks(Mnihetal.,2013)onthesuiteofgamesintheArcadeLearning
Environment(Bellemareetal.,2013)hassparkedanactiveinterestinDeepReinforcementLearning.
VanHasseltetal.(2016),Wangetal.(2015)andSchauletal.(2015)showedsignificantimprovements

sticktoasimplebaselineofthebasicDQNarchitectureandtrainingproposedbyMnihetal.(2013).
Hintonetal.(2015)firstproposedthatitmightbeeasierforsmallernetworkstomimictheoutputsof
abiggernetworkinsteadoflearningtheoriginaltaskfromscratch. Theyproposedthistechniqueof

Theideaofknowledgedistillationhasbeenappliedtothedomainofdeepreinforcementlearning

(2015)arenotableattemptsatexploringtheabove. Bothworkslearnconstrainedstudentnetworks

policydirectlygeneratedbytheexpertnetwork,orapolicyinferredfromtheQvaluesgeneratedby

contrast,westudytheeffectivenessofapplyingthesametechniqueswhenthestudentnetworkis

Addinginterpretabilitytothepredictionsofdeepneuralnetworkshasbeenanactivelystudiedarea

etal.(2017);Zhouetal.(2016)aresomenotableworkswhichinduceweaklocalizationofentities
